## The job, honestly sized

Twenty minutes to an hour, depending on how many client types you serve. You are not writing new
policy. You are writing down a rule your staff already apply correctly every day, which is why
Appendix A argues this is not an automation problem at all.

Three things make it work, and the third is the one almost nobody has:

1. **Organise by what the client is holding, not by what you own.** A client does not arrive
   thinking "I need the audit portal". They arrive holding a K-1. The page answers "I have this,
   where does it go?" so that the client never has to choose between your systems.
2. **Name your real tool, and link it.** SafeSend, Suralink, ShareFile, Box, CCH, LeapFile,
   whatever you already pay for. One destination per group. If a group genuinely has two, you
   have found a rule your team applies silently; write it down and the page gets shorter.
3. **Say how they know they are finished.** This is the line a client re-reads, and it is missing
   from every firm we looked at. "Upload your documents" has no end state. Three short bullets do.

## The template

Everything in brackets is yours to replace. Lines marked *(delete if not applicable)* are groups
most firms will not need all of.

### Sending us your documents

You have something to send us. This page tells you where it goes and how to know you are finished.

You will not have to choose. Find what you are holding below.

---

#### Tax documents → [your tax tool]

Anything that belongs to a tax return, in either direction. Your W-2s and 1099s, a K-1, mortgage
interest, tuition, brokerage statements, your completed organiser. Your finished return comes back
the same way.

**Send tax documents through [your tax tool] → [paste the link]**

**You are finished when** all three of these are true:

- Every form that arrived, by post or by email, is uploaded.
- You have told us about anything you sold this year.
- You have told us what changed: a marriage, a child, a move between states, a new business.

---

#### Anything we asked for in an audit or a review → [your request-list tool] *(delete if not applicable)*

If we are auditing you, reviewing your statements, or auditing your benefit plan, we send you a
request list. [Tool] holds that list and shows what is still outstanding. Work from the list
rather than sending files separately, so nothing is counted twice.

**Open your request list → [paste the link]**

**You are finished when** every item on the list has a file attached. If an item does not apply to
you, say so against it. A note counts as an answer.

---

#### Everything else → [your general tool]

Bookkeeping files, payroll records, month-end reports, entity documents, anything we asked for in
passing. Not a tax form, and not an audit request? Then [tool] is the right answer.

**Upload to [your general tool] → [paste the link]**

---

#### If you are becoming a client

Send these first and we will tell you what else we need:

- Last year's tax return, business and personal as applicable
- Your entity documents and your EIN letter
- The name and contact details of whoever kept your books until now

---

#### Two cases that catch people out

- **A document that could go either way.** A K-1 during an audit, for instance. Send it where the
  person who asked for it works: if it came up on a request list, it goes there.
- **Something large or unusual.** [Your size limit and who to ask.]

## Four things to check before you publish it

**Does every group end at exactly one link?** If a reader can reach two, you have moved the choice
rather than removed it. That is the single most common shape in Appendix A: 21 of 43 firms present
a corridor of doors with nothing saying which is yours.

**Is the link to your tool, or to a page about your tool?** One firm on our list describes both
its systems carefully and links ten of the vendors' own help articles, and has no door of its own
anywhere on the page.

**Have you written the inbound direction, not just the outbound one?** Three firms had an
excellent page covering delivery of the finished return — videos, an FAQ, e-signature — and
nothing at all for documents coming in. It is the same page, written once, for the direction that
ends in a signature.

**Would a client who has never met you understand it?** Read it as though you do not already know
which of your systems is which. Form numbers alone are not plain names; "your W-2 from each
employer" is.

## What we have not proved

We have written this page twice for real firms, in about two hours each. Neither has been sent,
because neither firm replied to the message offering it. So treat the structure as considered
rather than tested: it is what we think a good version looks like, and no client has ever used
one.
If you publish a version and it changes how many documents arrive complete the first time, that is
the number we would most like to hear, and it would be the first real evidence anyone has that
this page matters.
