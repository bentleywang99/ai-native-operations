# 7. Process archetypes and their redesigns

*Draft. Each archetype gets: how to recognise it, why it is usually slow, the target state, the
checkpoints that matter, and what it costs to run.*

Archetypes we expect to cover, based on the ideal customer profiles we sell into:

1. **Triage and route.** Inbound items classified and sent somewhere. Support email, applications, leads.
2. **Extract and file.** Unstructured document to structured record. Invoices, forms, contracts.
3. **Draft and review.** Produce a document a human approves. Proposals, reports, responses.
4. **Research and summarise.** Gather from many sources, condense for a decision.
5. **Monitor and alert.** Watch a stream, act or escalate on conditions.
6. **Reconcile.** Compare two sources of truth, explain and resolve differences.

Most real processes are two or three of these in sequence, and naming them separately is often
the first useful thing an assessment does, because it reveals that the slow part is a handoff
between archetypes rather than any single step.
